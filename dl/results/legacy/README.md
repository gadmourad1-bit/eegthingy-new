  published, verified SHA-256 identities, but inspection showed embedded
  trial-level predictions. Importing them would violate this bundle's
  aggregate-only boundary. The historical narrative remains in
  `deepnet/HEMIQ_FIELD_REPORT.md` and is not pooled with harmonized-v2 results.
- No authoritative aggregate-only GeoAdapt harmonized-v2 publication exists.
  That approved 6,495-job track was not run and is marked pending.
- HemiQ harmonized-v2 likewise has no formal score publication; its 45-job
  track is marked pending.

Nothing here should be interpreted as a missing score being zero or as a
cross-protocol comparison.
