# CardinalFBMS native-transfer verification

`independent_verification_lab.json` is the compact authoritative post-outcome
verification of 8,675 retained records from the prior GPU-box transfer study.
It passed all six independent checks and reports separate Cho2017 and
PhysioNet MI condition means. It explicitly identifies itself as not being the
frozen gate and must not be mixed with common-recipe results.

The large gate artifact and record tree are deliberately excluded: they carry
per-record paths/checksums and are unnecessary for this aggregate-only clean
bundle.
